## Context

What forced the decision: the constraint, the conflict, or the thing that broke.
State the facts a reader needs and nothing else — no history lesson.

## Decision

What we do, in the present tense. Be specific enough that a reviewer can tell
whether a future change violates it.

## Rejected alternatives

The options that were genuinely considered, each with the reason it lost. An
alternative with no stated reason is a decision nobody can revisit later.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it makes harder, and what a future author must now do
differently. Include the consequences you dislike.
