# Scope contribution and disagreement

Resolve each collaboration scope from existing Putnami scopes, projects and
native target/group selection, then record its exact project coverage in the
run's scope inventory. Reuse configuration inheritance and actual dependencies;
do not create a parallel registry or a new DAG unit. A scope target selects
itself when activated; a recursive target selects its descendants as well.
Record which selection the contribution covers instead of equating the two.

Use existing `putnami.architecture.json` declarations, when present, as context
for responsibilities and contracts. Their wire vocabulary does not define a
second collaboration identity. Audit labels and scorecard groupings remain
backlog metadata. Read settled decisions and consumer contracts before
proposing a new edge or replacing a shared capability.

The scope owner in a dossier is the consulted agent session, with a mandate
from the coordinator; it does not establish a new team or grant authority.
Consult that owner only when responsibility or contract judgment is needed;
ordinary scopes do not require architectural metadata or a permanent agent.

A scope owner is responsible for the scope's purpose, invariants, contracts
and capacity to evolve; preserving current code is not an end in itself.
Ask it for a distinct contribution only where that judgment is needed. Give it the objective, global constraints,
proposal revision and the exact relevant rules/decisions. Let it influence the
vertical decomposition instead of merely approving a completed plan.

Record a compact attributed note: scope, scope owner session, supplied context
references/digests, proposal reference, position and reasons tied to constraints,
accepted contribution, touched contracts, exclusions, acceptance and reservations.
`accepted`, `reserved` and `redirected` are positions, not votes. A reservation
names a concrete failure/capability loss and, where possible, an alternative.
A legitimate compatible solution can be accepted without a negotiation round.

Design agreement and realization acceptance are distinct notes. Bind realization
to the reviewed tree. Each opinion carries the examined proposal digest and
sorted supplied-context digests in its metadata, as defined in records.md.
A changed proposal/context invalidates its old note;
request an updated position while retaining the earlier one. Do not discard an
objection by replacing its scope owner. A receiving scope must accept a redirected
responsibility before the coordinator can consider the handoff resolved.

Negotiation can produce a compatible proposal, an attributed new opinion, or an
authorized arbitration. Preserve the original reservation and append the
resolution and authority reference. Do not change a historical refusal into an
agreement. Escalate decisions outside the mandate, including reversible choices
with material migration/strategic cost; keep independent tasks moving.

Local notes and hashes establish recorded context and declared authorship only.
Shared filesystem permissions do not stop forgery or deletion, and do not prove
that the scope owner understood every constraint. The pilot enforces evidence
consistency; separate writer permissions and hosted signed attribution remain
open runtime decisions, not prerequisites silently added to this skill update.
